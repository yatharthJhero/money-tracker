# Research notes

Use anonymized participant IDs (for example `S01`, `W01`). Do not commit names, contact details, account identifiers, statements, screenshots or raw financial documents.

Keep research lightweight and parallel to development:
- Aim for 3–5 initial conversations when practical.
- Ask about recent real behaviour and workarounds.
- Do not pitch features before understanding the problem.
- Record contradictory evidence.
- Treat early findings as directional, not statistically representative.

Never ask for UPI PINs, OTPs, passwords, CVVs or bank login credentials.
